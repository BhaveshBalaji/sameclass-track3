# SameSection

SameSection helps students choose classes through a shared planning conversation while preserving each student's needs.

## Language

**Chat room**:
A shared conversation space where students and SameSection exchange messages. It can contain successive planning sessions.

**Planning session**:
A bounded class-planning conversation with an explicit participant list and a selected semester.

**Participant**:
A student included in a planning session. Being present in the chat does not by itself establish participation.

**Student profile**:
A student's stated requirements, preferences, explicit flexibility, and unanswered planning questions, supported by that student's own statements or confirmation.

**Hard constraint**:
A condition that an option must satisfy for a particular student.

**Soft preference**:
A desirable characteristic that helps rank options without disqualifying alternatives.

**Unknown**:
A planning dimension for which the student has not supplied enough information. It does not mean the student is unrestricted.

**Shared option**:
A class option compatible with multiple participating students, with the covered students identified.

**Individual alternative**:
A class option for a student whose needs differ from the group's shared options.

**Course**:
A catalog subject such as CSE 575 that can be taught in different terms and sections.

**Section**:
A particular scheduled instance of a course in a term, with its own instructors, meetings, and enrollment information.

**Professor candidate**:
A search result that may correspond to a section's instructor, whose identity still needs verification before its reviews can support recommendations.

**Clarification**:
A question directed to a particular student to resolve information that affects planning.

**Assistant invocation**:
A student's explicit request for SameSection to respond, made by mentioning the assistant or replying to an outstanding clarification addressed to that student.

**Research task**:
A bounded investigation of a particular section, syllabus, or resolved instructor to answer the current request.

**Historical syllabus**:
A syllabus from an earlier offering whose term and instructor differ from, or precede, the offering under consideration. It supplies context with its applicability stated explicitly.
