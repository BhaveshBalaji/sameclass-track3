# ClassCue social video

## Style Prompt
A close, straight-on iPhone running dark-mode Messages. Warm and conversational, with crisp blue outgoing messages, graphite incoming bubbles and clean white typography. The phone is the hero, deliberately extending below the portrait canvas like a close-up screen recording. Large editorial headlines above it carry the story. Sparse blue light and softly drifting question marks establish registration stress; calm motion and clear course choices provide relief.

## Colors
- Canvas: #0A101B
- iPhone screen: #101113
- Incoming bubble: #303237
- Outgoing bubble: #087DFF
- Text: #F7F9FD; secondary: #BCC6D8

## Typography
Arial / Helvetica Neue for native Messages-like typography. 76px headline, 36–43px chat text, 46px voice captions. Monospaced tabular figures for times.

## Motion
0.3–0.5 second soft spring message entrances. Character-by-character compose animation, then a send swoosh. Finite three-dot typing pulses. Crossfades between related chat views and the original evidence crop. A slow camera push provides depth without competing with reading.

## What NOT to Do
- No invented ratings, professor quotes, live-seat claims or enrollment guarantees.
- No private assignment list or calendar event names in screenshot crops.
- No rapidly scrolling paragraphs or tiny full-screen screenshots.
- No flashing, glitch cuts, stock avatars or loud repetitive notifications.
- No claim that ClassCue is a separate messaging app or official ASU product.
